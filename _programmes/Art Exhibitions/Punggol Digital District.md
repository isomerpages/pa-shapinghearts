<p>ARTfully Able @ Punggol Digital District was a joyful celebration of abilities,
imagination and creative expression. With five vibrant zones, each brought
to life by a unique character and offering a unique and immersive experience.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/Artfullyable_PDDC1_449.jpg">
</div>
<p></p>
<p>The space came alive with live performances, storytelling, and live painting
sessions, where Shaping Hearts artists shared not only their talents but
also their creative processes in real time.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/Artfullyable_PDDC1_254.jpg">
</div>
<p></p>
<p>Families took part in hands-on activities and artist-guided craft workshops,
enjoying time to create and connect together. From Water Marbling on Pouches
to DIY Mosaic Fridge Magnets, everyone had a little something special to
take home.</p>

<p>Visitors also explored the exhibition through an engaging art trail by
HIDDEN, discovering hidden gems and creative stories along the way. The
trail culminated at Singapore’s largest 132-metre mural, co-created by
Shaping Hearts artists Simeon and Ezra and brought to life with the Punggol
Community, Punggol Digital District stakeholders, and Tell Your Children
Creative Studios.</p>

<p>We are grateful to everyone who took part in this shared journey of creativity
and connection. Thank you for being part of a community that celebrates
difference, collaboration and artistic expression in meaningful ways.</p>
